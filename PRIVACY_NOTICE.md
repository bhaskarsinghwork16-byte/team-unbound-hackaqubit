# Privacy Notice and Consent Policy

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-10-07

## Purpose
HealthScreen AI is designed as a preliminary screening and clinical referral support tool for rural community health camps. This application collects minimal personal information necessary to link medical screenings to patients and coordinate referrals.

## Data Collected
- **Identity Data:** Name, Age, Sex, Phone number, Address.
- **Clinical Data:** Risk factors, captured medical images (retinal/oral), automated quality scores, AI inferences, and referral status.

## Storage & Synchronization
HealthScreen AI operates in an offline-first capacity. 
- All data is encrypted at rest on the local device.
- Data remains strictly on-device until internet connectivity is established, at which point it is synchronized securely (TLS) with the central hospital/clinic database.
- Patient data is never uploaded to third-party AI cloud APIs.

## Retention Policy
- **Screening/Clinical Data:** Retained for clinical continuity per local medical regulations (configurable, default 7 years).
- **Security Audit Logs:** Retained for 1 year.
- **Images:** Retained for the duration of the screening lifecycle, subject to explicit consent if used for subsequent research.

## Consent Workflow
Before capturing any medical imagery, health workers must present this notice and capture explicit consent on the device.
Separate consent is required for:
1. Primary Screening & Referral
2. Anonymous Research / Model Improvement

## Withdrawal & Grievance
Patients may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the legality of prior processing but will halt future automated screening processing.
Requests for data deletion/correction can be initiated via the health camp administrator.
